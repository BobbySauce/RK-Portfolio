Student project

Nissan MID4
reimagined.

A Japanese supercar that never made it to production. Before the Honda NSX, the idea of a Japanese supercar already existed. The Nissan MID4 was one of those unrealized visions, clearly inspired by European mid-engined sports cars. The MID-II's dynamic, organically futuristic design set it apart from other Japanese cars. This project reimagines the MID for the modern day.

The MID4-II has a distinctive side profile. Its beltline, shoulder line and vent line up to create a dynamic, ribbon-like shape within the A-line. Together with its soft volumes and subtle dome hood, these details are essential to the MID's identity.

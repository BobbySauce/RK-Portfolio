KONSTRUKTIER

A STEAM Toy

One-week student project

A playful introduction to structural engineering.

The goal was to create an engaging educational toy. Konstruktier helps children discover how buildings stand, how forces travel, and how thoughtful construction can turn a small set of parts into something strong.

In collaboration with interior architecture students
Alexandra Gerbel · Lilli Weise

02 / How it works

Build, balance, and learn by doing.

Konstruktier is a wooden construction game for children aged four and up. With a limited set of components, players build portable structures while discovering load-bearing walls and how forces are distributed.

Challenge cards make every round different. Color sequences determine the order of the floors, while animal characters complicate the build: the giraffe needs two stories, the monkey makes the facade wobble, and the bear pushes a floor out of level.

As children solve each challenge, they discover how a building can take on complex forms—and become the architects of their own ideas.

KONSTRUKTIER

A STEAM toy

- Student Project -

Konstruktier is a wooden toy set for children aged 4 and up that playfully teaches the physics of building.

The goal: Construct a stable building using a limited set of parts. Learn to tame the forces that act on structures. Counterbalance and use load-bearing walls to solve challenging tasks, or feel free to make your own architectural dreams become reality.

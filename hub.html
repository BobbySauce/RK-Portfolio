Student Project

HUB
Your car, your settings.

Cars are often shared between family members, but each driver has different preferences. HUB explores how a smartphone can work as a personal key, bringing a driver's familiar settings into the car.

Personalized seat and mirror positions, interface themes, locations, and media help make each journey feel familiar from the start.

PHONES & MOBILE INTERFACES

01 / A personal connection

Your phone becomes your key.

For young drivers, a smartphone already plays an important role in the car. HUB builds on that familiar relationship: the phone identifies the driver and helps set up the vehicle for them.

Instead of starting from scratch each time, drivers can carry their preferred setup with them—even when the car is shared.

02 / Shape exploration

Designed for budget-conscious new drivers.

These sketches explore a clear, approachable interface for young and first-time drivers—keeping the design focused on the essentials to make a personal driving experience feel accessible.

Clear directions. Less distraction.
